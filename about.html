Get to Know Muyanalo Short Loans!

Muyanalo Short Loans is a short-term lending company with 8 years of experience in providing financial assistance to individuals facing unexpected expenses and short-term financial needs. The company offers loans ranging from R1,000 to R5,000, at an interest rate of 40%.

Loans are available to individuals aged 18 years and above who are permanently employed full-time and earn a minimum monthly salary of R15,000. Applicants are required to provide a valid ID, their latest payslip and three months' bank statements. Repayments are made through a convenient salary deduction method, allowing monthly loan instalments to be deducted directly from the borrower's salary.

Muyanalo offers a straightforward application process designed to make borrowing simple and convenient. The company values customer privacy, confidentiality and responsible lending while providing support throughout the application process. Operating hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:30, and Saturday, 09:30–17:00.

Your Privacy & Security Matter.

Frequently asked questions

How are payments made?

Repayments are made through a convenient salary deduction method, allowing monthly loan instalments to be deducted directly from the borrower's salary.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes. You can settle your loan early by paying the outstanding balance in full. Please contact us in advance to communicate and arrange the early repayment.

How long does the application process take?

The processing time depends on the completeness of your application and the verification of your submitted documents. The process takes a maximum of 1–2 business days. We will keep you informed about the progress.

What happens if my loan application is declined?

If your application is unsuccessful, you will be notified. This could be due to not meeting the loan requirements. You may enquire about the reason for the decline and whether you can reapply in the future.

Do I need collateral to apply for a loan?

No. Collateral is not required to apply. Applicants must, however, meet the eligibility criteria and provide the necessary supporting documents.
